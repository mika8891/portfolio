01 / About

Who's making this

I'm a game development student who ended up specializing in the parts of a game most players never think to name, the footsteps, the ambient hum, the texture on a mask that makes it feel cursed rather than cute. I like sound and surface as one problem: both are about selling atmosphere.

Across my projects I've worked solo and in teams, moving between roles as needed. from dedicated audio and texture work to picking up programming when a group needed it. I'm the person who catches the small inconsistency before it becomes a bug report.
